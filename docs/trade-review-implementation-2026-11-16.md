# Trade review implementation — 16 November 2026

This change extends the owner-operated app with reliable sharing, advance payments, durable ordinary edits, reusable packages, trade worksheets and owner team planning. It does not deploy database changes or payment services.

## Delivered workflows

- **Sharing:** issued copies are labeled separately from delivery. Invoice and estimate composers record link preparation, messaging-app opening and explicit owner sharing confirmation. Document screens can reload persisted sharing history; only the token-validated customer endpoint records a customer view. Canceling a composer never creates a delivery receipt.
- **Remote estimates:** expiring hashed tokens expose only a fixed customer copy. Acceptance binds typed name and explicit English/Spanish consent to that exact snapshot/hash and server time. Finger acceptance on the owner device now stores normalized bounded strokes and the accepted snapshot atomically. Lost acknowledgements can be retried without duplicate evidence. Evidence is immutable.
- **Deposits/milestones:** after accepting a public estimate, the customer can open its exact requested deposit payment. Owners can request deposit and labeled milestone advances from the job, share secure collection links and confirm cash received. These are advances against the agreed job total, not extra invoice charges. Only succeeded payments credit the final balance; processing payments remain separate. Stripe intentions use server reservations and idempotency keys. The database prevents overbooking, duplicate cash and cash collection while an online attempt remains unresolved.
- **Offline edits:** account-keyed snapshots and a serializable ordered outbox retain ordinary jobs, customers, hours, work, notes/voice text, receipts and estimate/invoice edits across restarts. Replays stop on failure and use idempotent insert IDs. Issuance and acceptance require server confirmation. The screen distinguishes local waiting, server confirmation and storage failure. Native photo persistence remains separate. Voice audio and receipt image persistence are unchanged.
- **Packages:** save/reuse owner-priced lines and customer scope, or create packages from Settings. Optional after-hours fees and pre-tax minimum adjustments remain visible editable lines. Packages can be reviewed and added to estimates or unsigned service-call work. Work-package lines add to logged hours/materials, so the UI explicitly asks the owner to check for duplication. A copied minimum does not silently recalculate after later edits.
- **Trade tools:** optional room/surface worksheets calculate net area and paint consumption; quotes use the owner's rate and require review before adding room-labeled lines. Coats affect consumption, not billed physical area: the owner rate must cover the chosen system. Reusable prep/finish/exclusion text can be copied to customer scope. Electrical records retain circuit, test/result, measurements, technician, date, permit/inspection and selected photo references, with a reviewed customer-language text handover. This is contractor evidence, not automatic certification. Worksheet/template saves require a connection; unsaved form content is explicitly described as unsaved. Photo references in text exports are IDs, not image attachments.
- **Owner operations:** workers, loaded labor rates, visit windows/assignments and overlap protection; existing billing hours can be attributed to workers with frozen internal cost rates. Budgets and actual costs show gross job contribution excluding sales tax, with no profit/margin claim while hours lack cost attribution. New internal-cost tables enforce owner-only access on the server. Worker planning records can be linked to helper accounts through the separate team-access workflow described below.
- **Helper access:** owner-generated one-use invitations are bound to an email and planning worker. Joining requires a signed-in account and fresh email OTP verification, independent of auto-confirmed signup claims. Helpers use assigned-job RPCs for their own hours, notes and photos; legacy business data, pricing, issuing/signing documents and AI remain owner-only. Logged helper hours also receive server-attributed loaded labor cost for the owner. Joining does not send an invitation automatically: the owner shares the link.
- **Credit variations:** negative changes are allowed with nonnegative resulting totals and checks against confirmed/pending collections; approved credit lines carry through to final invoices.

## Navigation

Settings → Service packages; Settings → Team & schedule. Jobs → Job profitability; Jobs → Measurements & completion. Unsigned service work and unlocked estimates expose package review. Signed-estimate jobs expose advance payments. The locked estimate builder retains a share action, so a prepared quote can be shared again.

## Migration/deployment requirements

Apply new migrations in order, including `20261116170000_quote_deposits.sql` , `20261116180000_team_access.sql` and `20261117100000_payment_integrity.sql`, then deploy the changed `pay`, `payments`, `stripe-webhook` and new `estimate-access` and `team-join` functions. Serve the new `/quote/[token]` and `/collect/[token]` routes at the configured HTTPS `KILN_PAY_URL`. Payment requests require the existing Stripe Connect configuration, publishable/secret keys and signed webhook setup. The app must not be released against an older backend: new payment fields, signing overloads and idempotent cash RPCs are required.

Helper onboarding additionally requires a working email-code delivery template/provider; the unchanged hosted default magic-link sender does not establish a working OTP flow. New online attempts deliberately reserve their amount; switching to cash or another method after starting an attempt is blocked until settlement. An explicit cancellation workflow is not included. Final cash recording requires a connection and explicit received-amount confirmation; it never blocks the ordinary offline outbox.

No secrets, hosted database, Stripe account, production deployment or real invitation was changed during implementation. Real wallet/bank settlement and native-device offline restarts still require environment-specific testing.
